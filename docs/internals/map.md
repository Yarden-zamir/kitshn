# Map

The map is the main view. These rules (K-5xx) hold the decisions and the limits of the map UI. The UI code and its captures are the record of the layout.

## Rules

- **K-501** The map shows every card and every circle. The "Show" menu hides a kind. Hidden kinds still count for their circles.
- **K-502** A drag of a card or of a selection into or out of a circle shows the share change before the drop. The drop applies it.
- **K-503** After each drop that changes a share, a toast names the change and has Undo for 10 seconds. The toast never covers the card that it names. For its first 500 ms, a toast button ignores a press that started before the toast showed, so a double press runs no Undo.
- **K-504** A drag of a circle by its head moves its cards with it. ⌘ (macOS, iOS) or Ctrl and a drag moves the circle alone, with the preview of K-502.
- **K-505** A resize of a circle shows the same preview as K-504 before the release.
- **K-506** A circle shows at most 4 member chips on its edge, then "+N". The circle head slides along the edge, at most 55° to each side, when a card covers its spot. Heads draw above cards. Known limit: two heads can still overlap. Revisit when circles often touch at their tops.
- **K-507** A card shows a chip for each circle that shares it now, with the circle colour and the level as text, so a touch screen and a colour-blind owner read it without a hover. Shares that ended or that a pause closes do not count. At most two chips show, then "+N".
- **K-508** Cards stack by group: the environments of one app, the pages of one thread, the files of one upload. A click opens a stack as a column, and no card covers the one below it. A drag of a stack moves all its cards.
- **K-509** New cards arrive in a tray outside every circle, so they start private (K-208). Cards that a recipe places (K-807) and files dropped on the map (K-1101) skip the tray. A card dragged from the tray uses the same drag, drop, refusals and Undo as a map card. A map card dropped on the tray moves there, out of every circle and its stack.
- **K-510** Multiselect: a box drag on empty space, Shift and a click, and "Select all of this kind". A selection moves as one.
- **K-511** The command palette (Cmd+K or Ctrl+K) finds every card, circle and action, so every action works without a drag, also on a phone.
- **K-512** The card panel shows who can open the card: each circle, its members, the level, and the address.
- **K-513** Zoomed out under 40%, cards become dots and each circle shows its counts by kind. The map never animates continuously, and a change plays at most one short transition.
- **K-514** Pan, zoom, fit and auto layout work as in the machine map. Each device keeps its own view in local storage. A fit shows every circle and card whole in the free area, down to zoom 0.15.
- **K-515** A machine card shows its live state only from data that the client already has (K-605), with no new traffic. Offline, it shows "seen <time> ago" and no load bars, because an old load is wrong. The rows have a fixed height, so a status change never resizes the card. One sign shows the worst problem of an online machine, and the machine panel lists each with its next step. Known limit: a provider that hangs while its bridge stays alive reports nothing. Revisit if owners meet a hung T3.
- **K-516** The map works on a phone: one finger pans, a long press opens the context menu and starts a drag, and two fingers pinch to zoom. The panel is a bottom sheet.
- **K-517** The map uses the tick3d look in light and dark mode (the `neo-brutalism` skill) and no native browser UI. Every icon button, control and short label has a tooltip. Needed information is never only in a tooltip.
- **K-518** Map state (places, circles, members, shares, stacks) lives in the kitshn database, never in Git (K-001).
- **K-519** Views next to the map: History, Queue, Inbox (K-1607), Seats, Usage (K-1303), Machines, Health (K-1604), Settings (K-1613), Tasks (K-1614), Clients (K-1602) and Library. Each view and each selected item has its own link, and `#map/<card id>` selects a card. Most views open in one panel over the right side of the map, which keeps its full width. Escape first clears a selection, then closes the panel. Known limit: a selection on the map does not change the link. Revisit when links to selections get shared often.
- **K-520** "Previews" shows a live, sandboxed frame of up to four site and page cards near the centre of the map. A frame exists only while it shows and takes no clicks until "Interact". A frame that does not load in 20 seconds shows "Preview not available", because a browser reports no error for a frame. A site behind Access shows only when the browser has its Access session, because the Access login refuses to load in a frame. A selected card can also open its own preview. A file gets one only for a type that a browser shows, because a frame downloads any other. A site frame gets `allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-forms`, which is safe only because each app has its own origin. kitshn never frames the admin host. A page or file frame gets only `allow-scripts` (K-1003).
- **K-521** The owner stacks cards by a drop on another card or with "Stack these". A stack holds two or more cards and may have a name. A card is in one stack at most. The cards of a stack sit on one point, so they have the same circles. A level override (K-205) and an end date (K-209) stay per card. When a stacked card's share ends, the card leaves its stack. Stacks by group (K-508) stay as they are.
- **K-522** The stack panel renames a stack, pulls one card out (in the same circles, so no share changes), and unstacks. Each of these is one revision with Undo (K-1502). A stack that enters a public circle asks first (K-210).
- **K-523** Cards, stacks, circles and the empty map have a context menu: right-click, long press, the menu key or a "⋯" button. The menu, the toolbar, the panel and the palette read one action list per item, so they never differ. An action that the rules refuse shows disabled, with the reason as its tooltip. A dangerous action has no confirm: it runs at once and shows Undo, or its label names what goes when it cannot be undone. In the palette, Enter runs a dangerous entry only when it is the only match or the owner picked it.
- **K-524** A card shows the icon of its item: the site icon (K-1112, K-1114), the GitHub avatar of a repo owner, the system of a machine, or the icon of a page or small image (K-1115). Without one, it shows the icon of the kind.
- **K-525** The owner drags the edges of the side panel and the tray to change their widths. They always leave at least 360 px of map. Each device keeps the widths in local storage. Known limit: a wider tray can cover cards of a saved view until the owner presses Fit. Revisit if owners resize often.
- **K-526** The toolbar has one "+" button for everything the owner adds. It reads the same list as the add items of the empty map menu (K-523).
- **K-527** When the owner unshares a stacked card without a drag, kitshn asks which part leaves: the card (which also leaves its stack) or the whole stack. It is a choice, not a confirm.
- **K-528** On a screen of 720 px and less, the toolbar is one row and the tray is a sheet. The page never scrolls sideways. A touch selects a card only as a tap that ends with no second finger.
- **K-529** "New circle for these" makes one new circle without members around the selected cards, so no access changes until the owner adds some. Every card keeps exactly the circles that it is in now. kitshn moves unselected cards out of the new circle, and takes the plan that moves fewer cards. It is one edit with one Undo. kitshn refuses when an unselected card cannot leave without a change of its circles. Known limit: the new circle is at most 3 times as wide as the widest circle. Revisit if owners group cards from far parts of the map.
- **K-530** `kit.<zone>/neo-brutalism` is a living style guide of the map, behind the map's Access application. It draws each primitive with its real component and fixtures, never reads the live state, and loads as its own script.
- **K-531** `kit.<zone>/concepts` explains the ideas of kitshn with the real components and fixtures, next to the style guide of K-530. It never reads the live state and loads as its own script.
- **K-532** "Organize" puts the cards in a grid in their area (the exact set of circles that holds them), so no card gains or loses a circle or a level. The server computes the share diff before it saves and refuses any change. Circles do not move. The grid depends only on the circles, the cards and their sizes, so a second Organize moves nothing. It is one revision with one Undo. An agent runs the same Organize on the cards that it may move (`POST /agent/organize`, `kitshn agent organize`). The map has no shortcut keys for actions, except ⌘Z or Ctrl+Z for the newest own change (K-1506). Known limit: after a new wake of the Durable Object, an agent Organize uses the size of a plain closed card. Revisit if owners organize with many open stacks.
- **K-533** A timed card (K-212) shows its end as a time of day, not a countdown, so it never repaints and never goes stale. The card panel, the menu and the palette set, extend and end the time. Each choice has Undo. The admin card has none of them.
- **K-534** The site wizard (K-1131) can add a game card (K-2001). It offers only online machines whose daemon has the capability `doors` (K-2002). It shows the port reasons of K-2001 as the owner types, and the server checks them again. "Remove game…" asks first, because it has no Undo.
- **K-537** The relationship lens: while one card is selected, the map draws lines to that card from what reaches it, and from it to what it reaches. A machine line comes from the check of its own token (K-107), so a line never shows more than Access lets through. Other members draw one line per circle. A provider kind gets no "what it lets you do" icons, because only its provider knows what its levels mean (K-005). The lens is read only and goes with the selection. The card panel lists the same lines as "Reach".
- **K-538** A card with a started action that takes time shows a progress sticker: a bar with the estimated progress and "<step>/<steps>". The Worker keeps the action and its steps in the snapshot, so every client shows the same. The expected time of a step comes from the last good run of that action on that card. The bar moves with one compositor transition per step and never animates continuously. A step that waits for a retry says "retrying". On failure the sticker stays until a member with control dismisses it. After a restart, an action that nothing drives any more fails as "Interrupted". A restore to a time keeps its steps in R2 (K-1822), because the restart rewinds the row of the action.
- **K-539** A share and a membership are two things. A card in the body of a circle is shared with it. A machine card dropped on the title of a circle joins it as a member and goes back to where the drag started. A member has no agent right until the owner sets one (K-1720). A public circle refuses a member. A change that adds to a circle, or switches it to "all must match", must keep someone that it admits now. So kitshn refuses a person with a machine or a device, because Access has no policy for that mix (K-402). This rule holds for every way in, on the server too. Every membership action also works without a drag.

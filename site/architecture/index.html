How kitshn works

kitshn is one brain on Cloudflare and a pair of hands on each of your machines. Cloudflare Access stands at every door, so a visit to your app never has to ask kitshn.

  • 🧠

    One brain

    A Cloudflare Worker and one Durable Object hold the map, every rule and all state.

  • 🚪

    Access at every door

    Your circles become Access policies. Cloudflare checks them at its edge, on every visit.

  • 🔌

    No open ports

    A small daemon on each machine dials out through its own tunnel, and runs your apps.

Circles and Access

Your map, compiled into Access

kitshn never checks a visitor itself. It writes your circles into Cloudflare Access, and Access checks every visit at the edge.

🪞

kitshn is the source, Access the copy

A daily check repairs any difference. kitshn names everything it makes kitshn … and never touches an Access object it did not make.

✂️

Taking access away is instant

Take someone out of a circle or lower a level, and kitshn ends their open sessions at once.

🔁

The tunnel checks again

A machine's tunnel checks the Access token too, so a wrong DNS record cannot skip the login.
